How to read this strategy

Each colored segment shows how often the averaged policy takes an action. Postflop, each bucket groups hands by their estimated showdown equity against a uniform random opponent. Its center is the mean estimated equity of the training examples assigned to it.

For a row with no accumulated policy, the CLI uses the visible cards’ equity and the current pot odds. Street shortcuts open the limped, checked-through line. Follow individual actions to inspect other pots and raise sizes.
